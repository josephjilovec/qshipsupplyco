No carriage, freight brokerage, customs service, inventory ownership or handling of client funds is offered by this concept.

Designed around recurring work

One category.
A manageable pilot.

For small distributors, growing online retailers and lean purchasing teams. The proposed first engagement would cover one recurring supply category, an agreed vendor group and a named approver.

  • Start with your existing suppliers and accounting records.
  • Agree response expectations and approval limits.
  • Review overdue actions, missing approvals and receiving discrepancies.
