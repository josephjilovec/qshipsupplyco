01 / Less chasing. More clarity.

Buying the supplies should not become a second job.

For a small distributor or online retailer, an ordinary reorder can involve a quote in one inbox, approval in a chat and a delivery date in somebody’s spreadsheet. The cost is often uncertainty: who owns the next action, and what is actually confirmed?

QShip’s first proposed offer is a managed coordination workflow for recurring, non-regulated business supplies. Start with an agreed supplier list and a single purchasing process. Add software only after the workflow proves useful.

03 / The first offer

The Purchasing Coordination Pilot

A proposed pilot covering one purchasing category, an agreed supplier group and a named approver. Your business retains purchasing authority and pays suppliers directly.

Proposed scope · Not yet bookable

An exception & closeout log

04 / A practical path

From an open question
to an informed next step.

  1. Define the purchasing boundary

    Choose repeat items, approved vendors, decision makers and required documentation. Agree on what needs explicit approval.

  2. Coordinate the open actions

    Track requests and supplier replies. Separate an estimate from a confirmed commitment and give each exception a person and a next step.

  3. Close the loop

    Reconcile received quantities and remaining issues with the client. Use the pilot record to decide what deserves automation.

05 / What this could look like
ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

A reorder, without the inbox archaeology.

Illustrative workflow: a retailer needs another run of shipping cartons. The brief records the existing specification, required date and approver. Quotes can be compared on the same basis, the approved choice is recorded, and receiving confirms what arrived.

  • Request → quote → client approval
  • Supplier confirmation → follow-up
  • Receipt → exceptions → closeout

07 / Good questions

A little clarity
before we talk.

Is QShip a carrier or freight broker?

The initial concept is purchasing coordination. It does not offer carriage, customs brokerage, freight brokerage or guaranteed delivery.

Will you buy stock or hold our money?

The proposed first pilot leaves approvals, supplier contracts and payments with the client. Inventory ownership and finance services are outside this initial offer.

Does this replace our accounting system?

No replacement is proposed. The pilot would work alongside existing purchasing and accounting records, with any future integration separately scoped.

What information is useful for an inquiry?

Share your industry, approximate purchasing frequency and one example of a recurring coordination problem. Do not send payment credentials, confidential contracts or live customer records.
