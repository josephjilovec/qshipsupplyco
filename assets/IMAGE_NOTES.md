# QShip image provenance

The three new photographs were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this redesign. They depict fictional representative scenes, not QShip-owned facilities, actual staff or an operating service. The existing hero image is retained as a supporting operations scene. No reference-site images were copied.

1536 × 1024 originals were exported as optimized WebP assets with 768 × 512 responsive variants. Web exports are technical resizes/compression only. Original generated PNGs remain in the task workspace. All website assets are in `assets/`.

New asset files: `receiving-hall.webp`, `supply-details.webp`, `purchasing-desk.webp` and their `-768.webp` variants. The retained image is `hero.webp` with `hero-768.webp`. The locally served Barlow Condensed font is licensed under the included `OFL-Barlow.txt`.

Create a premium cinematic industrial editorial photograph of an orderly modest supply warehouse receiving bay seen from a slightly elevated mezzanine, neat unbranded corrugated cartons and low pallets, a manual pallet jack parked safely, steel shelving and long bands of warm daylight from loading-bay windows. A calm credible small-business operation, tactile material detail, deep navy shadows, warm neutral cardboard, restrained pale mint painted floor markings. Wide 3:2 landscape composition with the logistics scene concentrated in the center and right; left quarter dark and uncluttered for white website typography. Beautiful light, authentic geometry and realistic equipment. No ships, no containers at sea, no fleet, no text, no logos, no watermark, no staged trophy facility, no glowing holograms. Concept photo, not evidence of a QShip-owned facility.
